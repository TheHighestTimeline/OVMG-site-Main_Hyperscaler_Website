Infrastructure built on conviction, not compromise.

OneVibeMediaGroup is a hyperscale infrastructure and media platform. With our strategic partners, we design power, site, connectivity, and sustainability together from day one, and we build the story and assets behind every project.

Making hyperscale infrastructure more accessible, sustainable, and certain.

The largest computing platforms in the world need infrastructure that can grow with them, and partners who can deliver it without the delays, misalignments, and compromises that define traditional development.

Integrated delivery means no gaps, no handoffs, no surprises.

OVMG was founded on a simple belief: the coordination gaps between site, power, connectivity, and sustainability teams are the primary reason hyperscale projects run late. With our partners, we close those gaps.

Our Values

What drives every decision we make.

01
Clarity over complexity
Infrastructure is complex enough. Our job is to make it legible, to our clients, our partners, and ourselves. We present information clearly, make commitments we can keep, and never obscure risk.
02
Sustainability by design
The decision: we designed the water loop before we designed the building.
03
Scale with integrity
Growing the portfolio matters, but not at the expense of execution quality. We take on projects we can deliver well, and we hold ourselves to the same standards at every scale.
04
Long-term thinking
Hyperscale infrastructure lasts decades. We make decisions that serve the full life of a campus, not just the development cycle: the right site, the right power strategy, the right partners.
05
Partnership over transaction
The best outcomes come from deep, long-term relationships. We invest in understanding needs before we propose solutions, and we build with partners rather than around them.
06
Leave it better
Our whole motto: leave every site better than we found it. Regenerative by design, in the communities and landscapes where we build.
