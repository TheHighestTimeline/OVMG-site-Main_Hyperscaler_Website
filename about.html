Infrastructure built on
conviction, not compromise.

OneVibe Media Group is a hyperscale infrastructure platform built by people who believe the next generation of compute deserves a better foundation — one where power, site, connectivity, and sustainability are designed together from day one.

To make hyperscale infrastructure more accessible, more sustainable, and more certain.

The largest computing platforms in the world need infrastructure that can grow with them — and they need partners who can deliver it without the delays, misalignments, and compromises that define traditional real estate development.

Integrated delivery means no gaps between teams, no handoffs, no surprises.

OVMG was founded on a simple belief: the coordination gaps between site, power, connectivity, and sustainability teams are the primary reason hyperscale projects run late. We built a platform to close those gaps permanently.

What drives every decision we make.

01

Clarity over complexity

Infrastructure is complex enough. Our job is to make it legible — to our clients, our partners, and ourselves. We present information clearly, make commitments we can keep, and never obscure risk.

02

Sustainability by design

NetZero is not a reporting exercise — it's an engineering constraint. Every OVMG project is designed with sustainability as a foundational input, not a feature added at the end.

03

Scale with integrity

Growing the portfolio matters, but not at the expense of execution quality. We take on projects we can deliver well, and we hold ourselves accountable to the same standards at every scale.

04

Long-term thinking

Hyperscale infrastructure lasts decades. We make decisions that serve the 20-year life of a campus, not just the 20-month development cycle. That means choosing the right site, the right power strategy, and the right partners.

05

Partnership over transaction

The best hyperscale outcomes come from deep, long-term relationships between developer and operator. We invest in understanding our clients' needs before we propose solutions.

06

Continuous improvement

Every project teaches us something. We build feedback loops into our delivery process so that each campus we develop makes the next one faster, more efficient, and more reliable.

A delivery model built around parallel execution, not sequential handoffs.

Site-first thinking

We start every project by understanding the site in full — its constraints, its opportunities, and its fit with the client's long-term infrastructure strategy.

Parallel workstreams

Site, power, connectivity, and sustainability planning run simultaneously from day one. This eliminates the sequencing delays that cause most infrastructure projects to slip.

Single accountability

One team owns the entire delivery. No handoffs between consultants, no blame between specialisms. OVMG is accountable for the outcome, not just our part of it.

Phased capital commitment

Our phased delivery model allows clients to commit capital incrementally while maintaining full visibility on end-state delivery timelines and costs.

Transparent reporting

We provide clients with the data they need — power, carbon, connectivity, schedule — in a format they can use directly in their own operational and ESG reporting.

Post-delivery support

Our relationship with a campus doesn't end at handover. OVMG provides ongoing monitoring, optimisation, and reporting support throughout the asset's life.
