Four commitments that define
every OVMG project.

01

Renewable Energy Alignment

Every OVMG site is evaluated for renewable energy accessibility — solar, wind, and grid-based clean power — and designed to maximise clean energy utilisation from the outset.

02

Transparent Carbon Accounting

We track and report embodied carbon, operational emissions, and offsets against a clear baseline — providing clients with the data they need for their own sustainability reporting.

03

Long-Term Pathway Planning

NetZero is not a single moment — it's a trajectory. OVMG builds a phased decarbonisation pathway into every project from day one, with clear milestones and accountability.

04

Infrastructure Efficiency

From cooling system design to power delivery efficiency, every infrastructure decision is evaluated through an energy and carbon lens to reduce operational intensity over time.

A repeatable framework that scales across every project.

Site-Level Design

Every campus is positioned and designed to maximise renewable energy access, minimise grid dependency, and reduce embodied carbon in construction materials and methods.

Power Strategy

Power planning starts with clean energy first — utility-scale renewables, on-site generation, and power purchase agreements are evaluated and integrated before fossil-fuel options.

Reporting & Verification

OVMG provides standardised carbon reporting aligned to the GHG Protocol and major hyperscaler ESG disclosure frameworks — so clients can use our data directly in their own reports.
