The Opportunity

Powered on site, built in phases.

Bennettsville pairs integrated natural gas generation with 1,000 MWh of battery energy storage across up to ten phases, giving AI and hyperscale tenants a path to reliable power, on site and in months, not years. OVMG advances the project alongside its strategic partners across land, power, engineering, and capital.
